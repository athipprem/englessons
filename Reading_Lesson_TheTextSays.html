UNIT 41 · LESSON 2 · SATURDAY

Why this lesson matters 🎯

Type it yourself — microphone off.

Yesterday you marked answers. Today you write them so they get both ticks — every time, with one sentence pattern.

Layer 1 · Answer the question that was asked
Layer 2 · Show the proof from the text
Layer 3 · Only when asked: your own life
Why a pattern?
In a real reading test you have no helper, and nobody checks your answer before you move on. A pattern is like a ladder in your head: you climb the same three steps on every question, so the second tick never gets forgotten. It works on a poster, a story and an opinion article alike.
ROUTE A · THE PICTURE

The building blocks 🧱

A good answer is made of blocks that snap together. Here is one answer from each kind of text.

A board or poster — How much does it cost to hire a child bike for one hour?

MY ANSWERA child bike costs 60 baht for one hour,
BECAUSE THE TEXT SAYSbecause the text says “Child bike — 60 baht an hour, 300 baht a day”.

A story — How does the driver feel about what Tariq did?

MY ANSWERThe driver is pleased,
BECAUSE THE TEXT SAYSbecause the text says “The driver nodded and smiled”.

An opinion article — Do you think phones are needed at dinner? Use the article and your own life.

MY ANSWERI disagree,
BECAUSE THE TEXT SAYSbecause the text says “ten minutes without a phone will not hurt anyone”.
ONLY WHEN ASKEDIn my own life, I put my phone in a drawer at dinner and we talk more.
Look at the colours
Blue is the answer. Orange is the proof. Green is your own life — and it only appears when the question asks about you.
ROUTE B · THE STEPS

The frame 🪜

You just saw the blocks. Now here is the same thing as one fixed sentence pattern — the frame. One move per line:

1ANSWER the question that was asked, in a full sentence. Use the question’s own words.
2PROVE it: because the text says “…” — copy at least four words that really show it.
3YOUR LIFE (only when the question asks what you think or know): In my own life, …
Read it back: did I answer this question? Do the copied words prove it?

Try it on a fresh question. Here is the board: Mountain bike — 120 baht an hour, 600 baht a day. Question: How much will it cost to hire a mountain bike for one hour?

1
Answer: “It will cost 120 baht to hire a mountain bike for one hour.”
2
Prove it: “…because the text says ‘Mountain bike — 120 baht an hour’.”
3
Your life? The question does not ask what you think, so this line stays empty.

Decision tree for line 3:

Does the question ask what I think or know?
No → stop after line 2
Yes → add “In my own life, …”
DRILL 3 · LOCATING

Read the board. Pick the answer that uses the frame with a quote that proves it. If you pick wrong, try again.

DRILL 4 · UNDERSTANDING

The story 👛

In a story the right quote is usually an action — something a character does. Pick the answer whose quote really shows it.
