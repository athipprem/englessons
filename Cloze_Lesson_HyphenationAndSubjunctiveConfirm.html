Subjunctive recap — the base-form rule 📋

When a sentence uses a trigger phrase like "insisted that," "demanded that," "recommended that," or "It is vital that," the verb that follows drops back to its plain BASE form — no "-s," no tense change — no matter who or what is doing the action.

Example: "The coach demanded that every player arrive early." NOT "arrives," even though "every player" sounds like it should take an "-s" verb.

🔑 Why this one slipped

"Arrive" without the "-s" can feel wrong out loud, because most present-tense sentences DO take an "-s" after a singular subject. The trigger phrase overrides that normal rule — spot the trigger FIRST, then go straight to the plain form without checking the subject at all.
