Why this matters 🎯

In a reading test, the first mark is for answering the exact question that was asked. Your proof box already earns the second mark. And in real life it works the same way: if a friend asks “Why were you late?” and you say “Bus.”, they still don’t know. An answer that fits the question is clear, quick and kind to the person reading it.

ROUTE A · THE KEY AND THE LOCK

Every question has a shape

Every question has a shape. Your answer has to fit it.

A question is like a lock. Only a key with the right shape opens it. Here are four locks and the keys that fit them.

Why…?
→
… because …
How did … change?
→
At first … but by the end …
Why does the writer …?
→
The writer … to show …
Which …?
→
…, because …

Left: the shape of the question. Right: the shape of the answer that fits.

Match the answer to the question.
Now you try. Tap the shape that fits each question.
ROUTE B · THREE STEPS

From the shapes to three steps

The shapes, as three steps.

1
FIND the question word — why, how, which or what.
2
START with the question’s own words.
3
FINISH with the shape that fits — because… · At first… by the end… · to show…
Read the question→FIND the question word→START with its words→FINISH with the shape

The steps on your own question

Question: Why does Grace slide her sketchbook across to Ravi?

2START → Grace slides her sketchbook to Ravi
3FINISH → add because… and your reason.
