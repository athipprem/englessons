Quick refresher on a rule you already know well:

"The teacher recommended that every student ___ their homework before dinner." Which fits?

Right — "recommended that" is the subjunctive trigger, base form always follows. Next →

One new mechanic — relative pronoun + preposition 🔗

ASMOPSS's cloze passages sometimes need a relative pronoun that carries its OWN preposition, because the missing preposition can't be added anywhere else in the sentence.

🔑 The test

Try reading the sentence with just "which". If it's missing a small word like "in," "during," or "on" right before the gap to make sense, the answer is "in which" (or whichever preposition fits) — not plain "which," "why," or "when".

"Sleep apnea is a disorder ___ people stop breathing for short periods multiple times a night." Which fits?

Urban Beekeeping

Over the past decade, keeping bees on city rooftops (1)___ increasingly popular in cities around the world. Supporters argue that urban beehives help pollinate community gardens and city parks, (2)___ would otherwise see fewer visits from pollinating insects.

Not everyone is convinced, (3)___. Some scientists worry that too many managed honeybee hives in one small area may actually compete with wild, native bee species for the same limited flowers, (4)___ the food supply thinner for everyone. Beekeeping associations (5)___ that new urban keepers space their hives carefully and plant bee-friendly flowers nearby to reduce this risk.

Whether the benefits outweigh the risks likely depends on how (6)___ a city's urban beekeeping programme is managed.

🔑 One more reminder
