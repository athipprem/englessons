🏫 SCHOOL READING — Unit 40
Why this matters 🌍

In a reading test, a teacher cannot see inside your head. She can only see what you wrote. When you can point to the exact words in the text, she can see you really read it, and you get the marks. This works in every subject, and any time you need to prove you are right.

Here is a line you wrote in Unit 39:

“she thought her friends would bully her because she was working at a noodle store”

If you can't point to it in the text, don't write it.

You understood the story. You got every multiple-choice question right. The marks went because the proof stayed in your head. Today you learn to put it on the page.

Now the same thing as three steps ✅

The highlighter works. Here it is as three steps you can use in any test, even when you cannot colour the page.

1
1 · FIND it — take the key word from the question and find it in the text.
2
2 · COPY it — copy at least 4 words from that sentence, exactly, inside “quotation marks”.
3
3 · SAY it — answer the question in a full sentence, say because, then add your quote.
If you can't point to it in the text, don't write it.

Watch it work. Question: Which two directions can a pilot steer in?

The point test 👉

Before you write a quote, ask two questions:

Can I point to these words in the text?
↳ No →
Don't write it.
↳ Yes →
Do the words answer the question?
↳ No →
Find a different sentence.
↳ Yes →
Write it! It is proof.

Here is the story again, and one question: Where did Mali's first boat sink? Three students answered. Tap Passes or Doesn't pass for each.

Drill 1 — Tap the proof 🖍️

Read the question. Tap the one sentence that proves the answer. If you miss, you get a fresh one.

Drill 2 — Does this quote prove it? 🔎

Each question shows an answer and a quote. Choose one of the three.

Drill 3 — Copy the proof ✍️

Copy at least 4 words from the text that prove the answer. Type them exactly as they are written.
