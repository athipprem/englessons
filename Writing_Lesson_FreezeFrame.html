Why this lesson matters 🎯

Every good story has one second that everybody remembers — the second it goes wrong. Writers who find that second, and stay inside it, make readers feel they were there. That is how you turn a "fine" story into a story people talk about at lunch.

Here are two pieces of your own writing, side by side.

Your science-fair story — the big moment box:

After that, they headed to the science room to build the actual. It took them about five hours, but the work paid off.

Your boat moment — five boxes:

SEE — I see a person on a boat.
HEAR — I heard a small whistle in the air.
SAY — I said “oh no let’s get out of here”.
SHOW — My legs were shaking …
What this tells us

When the page gave you a box for each check, you did all four. When you had one big box, they all disappeared.

The science-fair picture showed the second it went wrong — foam over the edge, the ribbon on the floor. Your story never got there. Today you learn to find that second, and to make the boxes yourself.

ROUTE B · THE ROUTINE

Freeze Frame, in four steps 🧊

The comic strip, as four steps.

1
FIND the second. Ask: which one second goes wrong? That is your big moment.
2
NOTE it. Four quick notes for that second: SEE · HEAR · SAY · SHOW.
3
WRITE it. Turn every note into a sentence, add one more, and stay inside that second.
4
CHECK it. Tap your four: can you find each one in what you wrote?

DRILL F1

Find the second 🔎

Four mini-stories. In each, pick the one sentence that is the big moment. You can try again until each is right.

DRILL F2

Stay or leave? 🚪

You have picked the big moment. Now every sentence you write must stay inside that second. For each sentence, choose Stay (still inside the second) or Leave (a later time or a new place).
