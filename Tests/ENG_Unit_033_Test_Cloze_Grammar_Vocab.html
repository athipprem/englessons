READ FIRST — for cloze: check whether a verb sits directly after a preposition (always -ing), and remember "arrive AT a decision" is a fixed phrase, not a rule to reason through. For grammar: after taste/feel/look/seem/sound/smell, use the plain describing-word, never the "-ly" form — a new verb doesn't mean a new rule. "Little" at the start of a sentence flips the subject and helper verb. "Much" needs "very" in front of it before a participle-adjective. For vocabulary: read the whole story once, then double-check your answer points in the SAME direction as the clue, not the opposite.
