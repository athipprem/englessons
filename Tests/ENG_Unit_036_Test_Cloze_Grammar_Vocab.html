READ FIRST — for cloze: check "arrive at" sentences ONE AT A TIME, don't skim for whichever "feels" wrong. Subjunctive triggers can be a reporting verb (suggested that) OR an adjective pattern (it is essential/vital/important/necessary that) — either way, base form, no -s. For grammar: Type-1 conditional — read the RESULT half first ("will" means the if-half must be present tense). "Much" needs "very" in front of it before a participle-adjective. After "Little did [subject]," the next verb stays in its plain base form. For vocabulary: read the whole story once, then double-check your answer points in the SAME direction as the clue, not the opposite.
