Every good answer has two layers — in reading and in stories.
Today you become the marker. Markers give ticks, so if you learn to see what a marker looks for, you can write answers that get every tick. (Stories work the same way: say what happened, then show it. That comes in the next unit.)
ROUTE A · THE PICTURE

The marker’s card 📋

A marker has a card with two boxes. Each box is one tick.

MARKER’S CARD
Tick 1 — did it answer the question?
Tick 2 — did it show the proof from the text?

Here is a tiny text and a question. Press Mark it under each answer to see what the marker does.

The text: The ice-cream van only comes on Fridays, after school. It parks beside the gate for twenty minutes.
Question: When does the ice-cream van come?
Answer 1: “On Fridays.”
Tick 1 — answers the question
Tick 2 — no words from the text
One tick. Right answer, but no proof.
Answer 2: “Whenever the sun is out.”
Tick 1 — does not answer the question
Tick 2 — no words from the text
No ticks.
Answer 3: “The van comes on Fridays, because the text says it ‘only comes on Fridays, after school’.”
Tick 1 — answers the question
Tick 2 — copies words that prove it
Two ticks. Answer plus proof.

Remember: one answer can fill both boxes, only one box, or neither.

ROUTE B · THE STEPS

How a marker marks 🔎

On the last screen you saw the card. Now here is how a marker fills it in — one move at a time.

1
Read the question. What exactly does it ask?
2
Read the answer. Does it answer that? If yes — tick 1.
3
Look for words from the text that prove it (or for “your own life” when the question asks about you). Do they really show the answer? If yes — tick 2.

The decision tree — follow it for any answer:

Does it answer the question that was asked?
No → 0 ticks
(it answers something else)
Yes → tick 1
↓
Are there words from the text that really prove it?
No → stop at 1 tick
Yes → tick 2 (2 ticks)
Words that earn nothing
Words that are in the text but prove nothing · a detail you made up · the answer said again with “because”. A marker gives no tick for any of these.
