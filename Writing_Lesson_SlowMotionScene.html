ROUTE B · THE FOUR STEPS

The four-step method 🪜

The cards showed you the pieces. Now here is the method, one move at a time:

1
Pick ONE moment. Not a whole day — one moment, like “the goal goes in”.
2
Write what you see and hear (or smell or touch).
3
Add one thing someone says, in quotation marks.
4
Show the feeling with a body action. Do not name it.
Then read it back: did I STAY on that one moment?

The decision tree for the last check:

Did my scene jump to a new place or a new time?
Yes → I left the moment. Go back and cut it.
No → I stayed. Five sentences or more? Great.
