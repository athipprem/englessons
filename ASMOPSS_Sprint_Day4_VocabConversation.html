Before We Start 🔍

Quick warm-up — pick the word that fits best:

"Don't believe everything she tells you, as she's rather prone to ___." Which fits?

Good — "prone to" pairs naturally with a HABIT or TENDENCY, which points straight at exaggeration. Next →

Formal/literary vocabulary — a notch above ISA's wordlist 📖

ASMOPSS vocabulary questions often use a more formal or "written" register than everyday speech — words you might recognise from books or news articles but rarely use out loud. The good news: your synonym-matching method (find the word closest in FEELING and STRENGTH, not just topic) works exactly the same way here.

🔑 Strength-matching, not just topic-matching

"Exhausted" isn't just "tired" — it's tired at MAXIMUM strength. When two options both seem topic-related, pick the one that matches the INTENSITY of the original word, not just its general area of meaning.

"It is disgraceful that children can get hold of drugs at school." Closest in meaning to "disgraceful":
"Europe should not try to mimic Japan; we must find our own path." Closest in meaning to "mimic":
"An honest person always tells the truth and does not cheat or ___." Which fits?

Idiomatic & functional conversation 💬

Conversation items don't test grammar — they test whether a reply makes sense as the NEXT thing a real person would say. Two things to check every time:

✅ Check 1 — does it ANSWER the actual question?
"Can you tell me how to get to the museum?" needs DIRECTIONS or a reason you can't give them — not a random fact about museums.
✅ Check 2 — does what comes AFTER it still make sense?
If the next line is "Don't worry about it, it's my treat," the blank before it must be someone hesitating about money or accepting an invite — work backward from what follows, not just forward from what came before.
"Do you mind if I sit here?" — best reply?
Donald: "Let's eat out, shall we?" ... Donald: "Don't worry about it. It's my treat." — What did the missing reply most likely say?

Mixed quick-fire — 60 seconds each 🔥

1. "The company ___ customers by selling old computers as new ones." Which fits?
2. "I should tell them they need more decorations." "___" "At least I can try. Maybe I can convince them." — best fit for the blank?
3. "After the climb, both men were physically exhausted." Closest in meaning to "exhausted":
4. Lydia: "Do you know who'll be taking over after Claudia retires?" Brian: "___" — best reply?

Day 4 done! 🎉

Vocabulary: match the STRENGTH/intensity of the original word, not just its topic. Conversation: check the reply actually answers the question AND still makes sense with whatever comes right after it.
